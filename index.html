Who You're Looking At

Anthony Suggs.
Antidote.

Anthony Suggs — Antidote The Foodie — is a Long Beach creator whose life reads like a comeback story with a soundtrack. Raised between Compton and Long Beach, he built a name as a rapper under the Antidote moniker, did time on a marijuana charge, came out homeless, and slept in his car.

Then he bought a food truck with a social-equity payout and turned it into a brand. The LA Times covered it in 2020. Now he runs cinematic, over-the-top food reviews — the Clamp bite, the Antidote Drizzle — alongside music, crypto, credit repair, and a wealth blueprint.

Everything on this page is one person. The reviews, the records, the crypto, the credit work, the blueprint: not separate businesses stacked up to sell you something, but the lanes one life actually runs in.

    “Believe Me” — What The Song Is Saying

    “Believe Me” is a reflective journey about awakening to a deeper understanding of yourself and the world around you. Rather than viewing life as something that simply happens to us, the song encourages listeners to consider that our beliefs, perspective, and inner dialogue play a powerful role in shaping the experiences we have. It invites us to look inward first, suggesting that lasting change begins in the mind before it appears in the world around us.

    As the story unfolds, the artist shares the emotional cost of personal growth. Questioning old beliefs, changing perspectives, and following an authentic path can create distance between you and people who no longer understand your journey. Yet the song presents this loneliness not as failure, but as a natural part of discovering a deeper sense of oneness, purpose, and self-awareness.

    Throughout the lyrics, recurring themes emphasize being intentional with your thoughts, emotions, and words, reminding listeners that where attention and energy are consistently placed often influences the life they build. Rather than waiting for others to validate or rescue him, the artist ultimately realizes that real strength comes from finding that source of peace within himself.

    By the conclusion, the message evolves from seeking understanding to offering it. The focus is no longer on convincing people to believe, but on becoming someone who inspires others simply by continuing to grow, love, and lead by example. “Believe Me” ultimately encourages listeners to reflect on their own consciousness, embrace personal responsibility, and recognize that genuine transformation begins within before it can ever be expressed outwardly. Whether interpreted spiritually, psychologically, or philosophically, the song leaves one central invitation: awaken to your own potential, and use that awareness to help elevate others.
