4. How to enter

Play Antidote Drizzle Bowl at antidotethefoodie.com/drizzle-bowl, finish a game, and submit your score with a display name on the end-of-game screen. That submission is your entry. The game is free and there is nothing to buy.

You may enter as many times as you like. Only your highest score in that weekly period counts. Entries generated by a script, bot, macro, automation, modified client, network tampering, or any method other than actually playing are void, as are entries that alter or forge score data. Sponsor's determination of what constitutes tampering is final.

5. How the winner is determined

The entrant with the highest verified score in the weekly period wins. This is judged purely on demonstrated skill in the game — no element of chance determines the winner.

Tiebreaker: if two or more entrants are tied, the entrant whose tying score was submitted earliest wins.

Sponsor reserves the right to disqualify any score it reasonably believes was not achieved through legitimate play, and to award to the next-highest eligible entrant.
