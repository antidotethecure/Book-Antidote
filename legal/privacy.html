This policy explains what information the Antidote Drizzle Bowl game (at antidotethefoodie.com/drizzle-bowl and antidotethecure.github.io/drizzle-bowl) handles, why, and what you can do about it. The game is operated by [[ENTITY]] ("we," "us"). You can reach us any time at antidotethecure@gmail.com.

The short version: There are no accounts and no sign-up. We do not ask for your email, phone number, address, or payment details, and we have never sold anyone's information. The only thing you actively give us is the short display name you type after a game, which we post publicly on the leaderboard next to your score. If you would rather not appear, don't enter a name — the game plays exactly the same.

1. What we collect

Information you type in

  • Display name — up to 12 characters, entered on the end-of-game screen. It is published on the public leaderboard. Please pick a nickname or gamertag rather than your real full name; see "Your name is public" below.
  • Your score and the week it was set.

Information stored only on your own device

The game saves a few values in your browser's local storage so it remembers you between sessions. These stay on your device, are not transmitted to us, and are cleared when you clear your browser data:

  • adb_name — the last display name you used, so you don't retype it
  • adb_best — your personal best score
  • adb_board, adb_board_cache — a local copy of the leaderboard so it still shows if you go offline
  • adb_last_first, adb_last_break — small bits of game state

Information collected automatically by the services that host the game

Like effectively every website, the servers that deliver the game and run the leaderboard record standard technical request data — IP address, browser and device type, referring page, and date and time. We use this only to serve the game, keep the leaderboard working, and block abuse. We do not use it to build a profile of you.

What we never collect

No account or password, no email address or phone number, no mailing address, no payment or card details, no precise location, no contacts or photos, and no cross-site advertising identifiers. The game shows no third-party ads and contains no advertising trackers.

If you are in the UK or EEA, our lawful basis is legitimate interests for operating and securing the game, and consent for publishing the display name you choose to submit — a submission you make voluntarily and can ask us to undo at any time.

3. Your name is public

The whole point of the leaderboard is that it is visible to everyone, so anything you type as a display name becomes public and may be seen, screenshotted, and shared. Do not enter your full real name, a school name, a home address, a phone number, or anything else you would not want strangers to see. We can remove any entry — just email us (see section 8).

5. Children

The game is intended for players 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has submitted a name to the leaderboard, email us and we will delete it promptly. If you are in the UK or EEA, you should be 16 or older to submit a name, or have a parent's permission.

Prizes, where offered, are open only to entrants 18 and older. See the Official Rules.

6. How long we keep it

Leaderboard entries — display name, score, and week — are kept indefinitely so past weeks and the all-time board stay intact, unless you ask us to remove yours. Server request logs are kept only as long as our hosting providers retain them in the ordinary course, generally a short period.

7. Security

The game is served over HTTPS and we hold very little about you in the first place, which is the strongest protection available. That said, no method of transmission or storage on the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Don't submit. Skip the name screen and nothing is sent to the leaderboard.
  • Get your entry removed. Email antidotethecure@gmail.com with the display name and roughly when you played, and we'll delete it.
  • Clear the device data. Clearing your browser's site data for the game removes everything stored locally.

Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights — to know what we hold, to get a copy, to correct it, to delete it, to object to or restrict how we use it, and not to be discriminated against for exercising any of them. California residents have these rights under the CCPA/CPRA; UK and EEA residents under the UK GDPR and GDPR. To exercise any of them, email us. We will respond within the time the applicable law allows, and we will not charge you for it. Because we hold no account for you, we may need you to tell us the display name and approximate date so we can find the right entry.

UK and EEA residents also have the right to complain to their local data protection authority.

9. International players

We operate from the United States, and the services listed in section 4 process data in the United States. If you play from outside the US, you understand that the limited information described here is handled in the US.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes will also be noted on the game's start screen. Continuing to play after a change means you accept the updated policy.
