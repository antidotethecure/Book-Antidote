Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 September 2026

This policy covers antidotethefoodie.com and Antidote Shorts Bot, a private publishing tool used by Anthony Suggs (“Antidote”) to upload his own video content to his own social accounts.

The short version. Antidote Shorts Bot is a single-operator tool. It publishes Antidote’s own videos to Antidote’s own channels. It has no users other than its operator, collects nothing from visitors to this website, and sells nothing to anyone.

Who this covers

Antidote Shorts Bot is not a consumer product. It is a personal automation tool with exactly one authorized account: the operator’s. It does not offer sign-in, does not create accounts, and is not made available to third parties.

What the tool accesses

When the operator authorizes the tool against his own Google Account, it receives access limited to these scopes:

  • youtube.upload — upload videos to the operator’s own channel.
  • youtube.force-ssl — manage the operator’s own video metadata (titles, descriptions, tags) over an encrypted connection.
  • yt-analytics.readonly — read the operator’s own channel performance data.
  • yt-analytics-monetary.readonly — read the operator’s own revenue figures.

All four apply solely to the operator’s own channel. The tool never requests access to anyone else’s account, and it cannot read other people’s private data.

YouTube API Services

Antidote Shorts Bot uses YouTube API Services. By authorizing it, the operator is also agreeing to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google’s handling of any data reached through those APIs is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access granted to this tool can be withdrawn at any time from the Google security settings page at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revocation takes effect immediately and stops all further publishing.

How data is stored

  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the operator’s personal computer, in a file readable solely by the operator’s account.
  • Tokens are never transmitted anywhere except to Google’s own authentication endpoints, over HTTPS, to obtain and refresh access.
  • Analytics figures the tool retrieves are written to local log files on that same machine and are used only for the operator’s own reporting.
  • Nothing is uploaded to any third-party server, database, or analytics service.

What is never done

  • No data is sold, rented, or traded.
  • No data is shared with advertisers, data brokers, or ad networks.
  • No data is used to train machine-learning or AI models.
  • No YouTube data is transferred to any third party.

This website

antidotethefoodie.com is a static website. It sets no tracking cookies and runs no advertising or analytics trackers of its own. Pages may embed content from third-party platforms — YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Gumroad, OpenStreetMap tiles — and those providers apply their own privacy policies to anything they receive when their content loads.

If you contact the operator by email or phone, that message is used only to reply to you.

Data retention

OAuth tokens are kept only while the automation is running and are replaced whenever they are renewed. Old copies are deleted automatically. Revoking access, or deleting the local token file, removes them entirely.

Children

This tool is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes

Any update to this policy will be posted on this page with a revised date above.
