"Open app" doesn't work in every browser, and that's outside this page's control. DuckDuckGo, Opera, Arc Search, Brave and similar browsers often block handing off to other apps on purpose (a privacy feature), and no link on any page can override that. Try Safari or Chrome for the direct jump — or on any browser, tap Copy handle, open Instagram yourself, and paste the handle into search. That always works.
