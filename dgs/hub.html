Add a guard

Each guard gets their own login. Send them the app link plus their username and the one-time password. They make their own password the first time they sign in. iPhone: open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.

Add a site

Tip: tap the map on the Live tab to drop the site's pin. Guards can only clock in inside the red circle — keep it tight (75–150 m) so nobody can clock in from down the street.

Add a client

The business that books DGS. They get their own login at : they see the guards coming (name, photo, guard card, on-site status), request guards, pay their bill by card or bank through Stripe, and read daily reports for their locations. Link their locations under Sites and set the bill rate there.

Pay settings

California: 10-minute rest breaks must be paid; a 30-minute meal break can be unpaid if the guard is fully relieved. Ask your payroll company.

House rules

Every guard has to read and tap “I agree” before they can use the app. Change the rules and everyone has to agree again. Who agreed (and when) shows in Back office.

Add a manager

Managers get their own login to this command center: book guards on shifts, take them off, clock them out, see attendance and the login log. Only the owner can add or remove managers.
